# Contributing

Small, focused improvements are welcome. Open an issue describing the problem
before starting a large change. Include reproduction steps, your operating
system and the expected result when reporting a bug.

Follow the setup instructions in README.md. Include screenshots for visual
changes and describe the checks you ran in your pull request. Keep credentials,
local agent settings and personal data out of commits. Use synthetic fixtures.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is covered by the license
in LICENSE. Third-party dependencies retain their own licenses.
